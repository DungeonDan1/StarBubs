Meet Melody

Qualified Teacher · Mum of Two · Founder

I've spent years working in primary education, and becoming a mum changed everything about how I saw early learning. When my own two came along, I wanted classes that were genuinely thoughtful, structured around how babies and toddlers actually develop, not just noise and chaos.

So I created StarBubs. Classes I'd want to take my own children to, where little ones are met at their stage and parents feel just as welcome as their babies. Every session follows the same familiar structure, and every activity is designed to match your child's age and development.

Your baby can cry, sleep, crawl away, or sit and watch. All of that is fine. Come as you are, I'll take care of the rest.
