Frequently Asked
Questions

We know trying something new with your little one can feel like a big step. Here are the things parents ask us most — and if your question isn't here, just get in touch.

Every session follows the same structure so your baby knows what to expect. We start with hello songs, move into themed sensory activities and interactive play, then finish with a calm wind-down. Sessions last around 45 minutes and are led by Melody — a qualified primary school teacher and mum of two.

That's completely fine — it happens all the time and nobody will bat an eyelid. You're welcome to step out, feed, change, or just cuddle them through it. There's zero pressure for your baby to "perform" or join in. Most babies settle once they get used to the environment, and some just need a few sessions to feel comfortable.

We have three classes based on your child's stage of development: StarGazers for non-mobile babies (0–6 months), StarCruisers for crawlers and explorers (6–12 months), and StarChasers for walkers and toddlers (1–3 years). If your child is between stages, just drop us a message and we'll help you pick.

Yes — spaces are limited to keep the sessions small and personal, so we ask everyone to book ahead. You can book online through our website in just a couple of taps. If a class is full, you can join the waitlist and we'll let you know as soon as a spot opens up.

Babies under 8 weeks old attend completely free — it's a great way to try a session with no commitment. For older babies and toddlers, we offer trial sessions at a reduced rate so you can see if it's a good fit before committing to a block.

Just yourself and your little one! Everything you need for the session is provided. We'd suggest bringing a drink and snack for yourself, and whatever you'd normally take out with you for your baby (nappies, wipes, a change of clothes if they're at the messy play stage). Comfy clothes are a good idea too — you'll be on the floor!

Absolutely! Mums, dads, grandparents, carers — everyone's welcome. One adult per child is included in the booking. If you'd like to bring a second adult along, just let us know and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Our classes are designed for specific age groups, so we generally ask that siblings attend their own age-appropriate class. If your older child is of school age and it's the holidays, just drop us a message and we can chat about it — we're always flexible where we can be.

Life with little ones is unpredictable — we completely understand. If you need to miss a session, just let us know. We'll always try to offer a catch-up session where possible. For cancellations, check your booking confirmation for the specific terms, but we keep things as flexible as we can.

We run classes in two locations across Somerset: Bridgwater (Westfield Church Hall) and Taunton (Priory Community Centre). Both venues have parking, changing facilities, and easy pushchair access. Head to our locations page for full details and timetables.
