Three classes, three stages

Find the right class for your little one

Every child develops at their own pace. Our three classes are designed around age and stage, so your baby gets exactly the right level of stimulation, interaction, and fun.

0-6 months
Newborns & Pre-Crawlers

StarGazers

Designed for the tiniest members. Soft lights, gentle sounds, and calm sensory experiences. Your baby explores at their own pace while you bond and connect with other parents.

  • Sensory exploration — light-up toys, textured fabrics, soft mirrors
  • Gentle music & song — lullabies, nursery rhymes, calming melodies
  • Tummy time stations — supported play for physical development
  • Parent bonding time — relaxed environment, meet other new parents
Book StarGazers View Timetable
Crawlers & Explorers

StarCruisers

Your baby is on the move and ready to explore. These sessions match their growing curiosity with tunnels, textures, and interactive play — all in a safe, padded environment.

  • Crawling adventures — tunnels, ramps, and soft obstacles
  • Tactile sensory play — water beads, sand trays, textured mats
  • Music & instruments — shakers, drums, and action songs
  • Social interaction — parallel play with other babies their age
Book StarCruisers View Timetable
6-12 months
1-3 years
Walkers & Toddlers

StarChasers

High energy, big adventures. Toddlers run, jump, dance, and explore themed worlds every week. Music, movement, and props keep them engaged from start to finish.

  • Themed adventures — safari, under the sea, circus, and more every week
  • Music & dance — action songs, instruments, parachute play
  • Physical play — obstacle courses, inflatables, soft play equipment
  • Creative activities — messy play, role play, imaginative props
