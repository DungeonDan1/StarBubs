# StarBubs – Company Context

## Overview
StarBubs is a baby and toddler class provider operating in the South West of the UK, primarily in Bridgwater and Taunton. The business delivers structured, themed sessions designed to support early development while creating a calm, welcoming environment for both babies and parents.

StarBubs is not positioned as entertainment alone. It sits at the intersection of:
- Early years development
- Parent wellbeing
- Community building

The core objective of the business is to fill classes consistently by building trust, reducing parent anxiety, and making booking simple and accessible.

---

## Founder & Face of the Brand

### Melody (Founder & Class Leader)
- Qualified primary school teacher
- Mother of two
- Founder and operator of StarBubs

Melody is the central brand asset.

She represents:
- Trust (qualified teacher)
- Relatability (mum)
- Warmth (friendly, welcoming personality)
- Authority without intimidation

### Brand Role of Melody
Melody is not just an instructor — she is the brand.

Her presence should:
- Reduce anxiety for first-time parents
- Explain what happens clearly
- Make classes feel safe and approachable
- Be visible across video, website, and social content

## Brand Positioning

### Core Position
“A calm, welcoming baby and toddler class led by a qualified teacher.”

### Key Differentiators
- Structured sessions (not chaotic or random)
- Age-appropriate activities
- Teacher-led (not entertainer-led)
- Relaxed, no-pressure environment
- Strong parent community element

### What StarBubs is NOT
- Not loud, chaotic “kids entertainment”
- Not overly corporate or clinical
- Not overly “magical” or cliché-led branding
- Not high-pressure or commitment-heavy

---

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience
Parents of babies and toddlers (0–3 years), specifically:
- New mums (especially first-time parents)
- Parents in early weeks/months feeling isolated
- Parents seeking structured, safe activities

### Emotional Drivers
- Anxiety about first class
- Desire to “get out of the house”
- Need for social interaction
- Wanting developmental benefit for baby
- Desire for routine and structure

### Key Concerns to Address
- “What if my baby cries?”
- “What if they don’t join in?”
- “Will it be overwhelming?”
- “Will I feel awkward?”

## Classes Overview

StarBubs classes are structured and themed, with each session following a consistent format.

### Class Types

#### StarGazers (Non-Mobile)
- Newborns and young babies
- Sensory exploration
- Gentle, calm environment
- Parent bonding focus

#### StarCruisers (Crawlers)
- Babies beginning movement
- More active engagement
- Sensory and physical interaction

#### StarChasers (Walkers)
- Toddlers
- High energy
- Movement, music, interaction

---

## Session Structure

Each class follows a predictable flow:

1. Welcome & Hello Songs
2. Themed Activities & Sensory Play
3. Interactive Elements (music, props, movement)
4. Exploration Station (free play)
5. Calm Wind Down

### Key Principle
Structure creates reassurance for parents.

- A strong personal brand (Melody)
